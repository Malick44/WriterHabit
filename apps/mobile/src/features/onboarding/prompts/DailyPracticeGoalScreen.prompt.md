# Daily Practice Goal Screen Design Prompt

## Target User
Student (Grades 1-12)

## Goal
Design a futuristic, highly motivating Daily Practice Goal selection screen. The AI agent must generate at least 5 design variants.

## Prompt

You are a senior principal product designer and mobile UI/UX specialist.
Design an elegant, premium, futuristic mobile app screen for WriterHabit AI (onboarding step 5 of 6: Daily Practice Goal). The design must feel motivating, gamified, and visually stunning.

### Futuristic Design & Aesthetic Directives:
- **Motivating & Gamified UI**: Select time goals (e.g., 10, 15, 20 minutes) presented as energy levels, orbit paths, or glowing power cores.
- **Visual Rewards**: Preview of streak multipliers or glowing reward indicators depending on selection.
- **Modern Styling**: Curved glassmorphic paths, glowing accents, neon teal/green progress indicators, and rich dark-mode contrast.

### Screen Purpose & Requirements:
- **Progressive Flow**: Progress indicator representing Step 5/6.
- **Copy**:
  - Title: "Determine Daily Velocity"
  - Subtitle: "Continuous practice constructs skill. Set your daily session duration."
- **Interactive Controls**: Time selection choice cards. Active options light up with glowing charge bars or pulse animations.
- **CTA Actions**:
  - Primary CTA: "Lock Goal" (Continue).
  - Secondary CTA: "Rewind" (Back).

---

### Task: Generate At Least 5 Design Variants
You must generate at least 5 distinct futuristic, premium design concepts for this screen:
1. **Glowing Power Cores**: Glowing vertical cylinders representing minutes, charge level pulses to reflect selected energy.
2. **Orbit Ring Slider**: A circular metallic track with a glowing planet indicator, slide to rotate and adjust practice time.
3. **Glassmorphic Bento Grid**: Frosted glass modules with neon green success bars and embedded animated streak icons.
4. **Neon Hologram Timer**: A retro-futuristic digital clock format where numbers glow and warp elegantly during selection.
5. **Spatial Depth Blocks**: Elevated blocks that recess dynamically when tapped, displaying glowing interior light trails.

For each variant, provide detailed layout, styling tokens, and gamification feedback.
