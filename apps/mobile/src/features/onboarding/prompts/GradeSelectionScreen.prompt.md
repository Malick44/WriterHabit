# Grade Selection Screen Design Prompt

## Target User
Student (Grades 1-12)

## Goal
Design an elegant, futuristic, and highly engaging Grade Selection screen that groups elementary, middle, and high school grades. The AI agent must generate at least 5 design variants.

## Prompt

You are a senior principal product designer and mobile UI/UX specialist. 
Design an elegant, premium, futuristic mobile app screen for WriterHabit AI (onboarding step 2 of 6: Grade Selection). The design must feel high-fidelity, interactive, and visually stunning.

### Futuristic Design & Aesthetic Directives:
- **Rich Aesthetics**: Utilize advanced layouts such as glassmorphism, dynamic organic background gradients, and smooth spatial elevation.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Clearly group grade levels into three categories: Elementary (Grades 1-5), Middle School (Grades 6-8), and High School (Grades 9-12), using distinct visual zones.
- **Adaptive Visuals**: Elementary should feel friendly, large, and gamified; Middle School should feel tech-forward and structured; High School should feel clean, premium, and sophisticated.
- **Typography & Color**: Clean fonts (e.g., Space Grotesk or Outfit), tailored color gradients, and glowing accents indicating current selection.

### Screen Purpose & Requirements:
- **Progress Indicator**: A neon progress bar representing Step 2/6.
- **Copy**:
  - Title: "Calibrate Your Level"
  - Subtitle: "The writing coach adapts its intelligence to your grade level."
- **Interactive Controls**: Grouped grade selection modules. The active grade card must feature glowing borders, color-shifting gradients, and interactive feedback.
- **CTA Actions**:
  - Primary CTA: "Proceed to Calibration" (Continue).
  - Secondary CTA: "Rewind" (Back).

---

### Task: Generate At Least 5 Design Variants
You must generate at least 5 distinct futuristic, premium design concepts for this screen:
1. **Interactive Nebula Grid**: Cosmic backdrop with glowing nebula blobs; grades are clustered like star constellations that glow when selected.
2. **Neon Glass-Tabs**: Layered frosted-glass tabs for Elementary/Middle/High, with neon blue, teal, and magenta color-codes.
3. **Futuristic Dashboard Bento**: Clean dark-mode titanium tiles with minimal typography, using micro-animations and glowing indicators for the selected grade.
4. **Holographic Floating Wheel**: A 3D-feeling scroll wheel or carousel with a glowing selection gate in the center.
5. **Cyber-Academy Blocks**: Bold, high-contrast neomorphic block layout using rich shadows, bold neon yellow triggers, and playful visual accents for younger grades.

For each variant, provide detailed layout descriptions, styles, CSS tokens, and grade-band adaptations.
