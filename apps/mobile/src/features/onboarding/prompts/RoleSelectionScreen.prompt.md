# Role Selection Screen Design Prompt

## Target User
Student (Grades 1-12) / Parent

## Goal
Design a futuristic, elegant, and immersive Role Selection screen for onboarding. The UI must break away from generic form layouts, using premium, high-fidelity styles that wow the user. The AI agent must generate at least 5 design variants.

## Prompt

You are a senior principal product designer and mobile UI/UX specialist. 
Design an elegant, premium, futuristic mobile app screen for WriteWise AI (onboarding step 1 of 6: Role Selection). The design must feel high-fidelity, extremely polished, and visually stunning.

### Futuristic Design & Aesthetic Directives:
- **Rich Aesthetics**: Avoid generic styles, solid grey cards, or standard system defaults. Incorporate sleek dark/light adaptive mode, smooth color-shifting gradients, glow/reflection effects, or dynamic glassmorphism (layered translucent sheets with background blur).
- **Harmonious Palette**: Use sophisticated tailored HSL/custom color schemes (e.g., deep cyber-indigos, glowing cyan accents, warm golden amber highlights, and neon violet glows).
- **Modern Typography**: Use clean premium typography (e.g., Outfit, Inter, or Space Grotesk) with advanced spatial contrast and weight.
- **Dynamic Elements & Motion**: Suggest micro-interactions, subtle float animations, and reactive hover/press glow states.
- **Academic Integrity**: Highlight coaching, skill building, and guidance over shortcuts.

### Screen Purpose & Requirements:
- **Progressive Flow**: A glowing neon progress indicator representing Step 1/6.
- **Copy**: Futuristic headers and welcoming microcopy:
  - Title: "Activate Your Role"
  - Subtitle: "Choose your access key to enter the writing environment."
- **Interactive Controls**: A set of immersive interactive components representing user roles (Student). The active selection should feature a vibrant glowing border, dynamic particle indicators, or depth-shifting elevations.
- **CTA Actions**: 
  - Primary CTA: "Launch Journey" (Continue button - animated state).
  - Secondary CTA: "Terminate Connection" (Sign Out - ghost glass button).

---

### Task: Generate At Least 5 Design Variants
You must generate at least 5 distinct futuristic, premium design concepts for this screen:
1. **Glassmorphism Hologram**: Blurry translucent panels with glowing neon cyan borders floating over a fluid cosmic backdrop.
2. **Minimalist Cyber-Bento**: Highly structured grid layout featuring sleek dark titanium blocks, clean white typography, and vibrant purple micro-accent lines.
3. **Fluid Gradient Orbit**: Soft, abstract organic blobs drifting in the background, with interactive elements that color-shift and glow upon hover or touch.
4. **Retro-Futurism Academy**: A neon-cyberpunk school theme using high-contrast glowing grid-lines, pixel-perfect borders, and bright neon yellow highlights.
5. **Spatial Depth Layering**: Interactive sheets that physically elevate and cast smooth, multi-colored ambient drop shadows when focused.

For each variant, provide:
- A detailed layout and visual style description.
- Key CSS/styling values (colors, gradients, drop shadows, borders, animations).
- Component breakdowns and accessibility/motion design notes.
