# Writing Confidence Screen Design Prompt

## Target User
Student (Grades 1-12)

## Goal
Design an elegant, futuristic Writing Confidence screen that gauges how the student feels about writing to adapt the coach's tone. The AI agent must generate at least 5 design variants.

## Prompt

You are a senior principal product designer and mobile UI/UX specialist.
Design an elegant, premium, futuristic mobile app screen for WriteWise AI (onboarding step 4 of 6: Writing Confidence). The design must feel high-fidelity, emotionally intelligent, and visually stunning.

### Futuristic Design & Aesthetic Directives:
- **Emotional & Visual Connection**: The selection should evoke confidence. Use color gradients that transition from a soft, cautious warm amber (Getting Started) to an empowered, glowing emerald/cyan (Ready to Shine).
- **Elegant Components**: Floating cards, glassmorphic sliders, or neomorphic dials rather than basic lists.
- **Typography & Styling**: Clean typography (e.g., Space Grotesk), micro-animations, and soft-glowing backdrops.

### Screen Purpose & Requirements:
- **Progress Indicator**: A neon progress bar representing Step 4/6.
- **Copy**:
  - Title: "Sync Your Confidence"
  - Subtitle: "This calibrates your writing coach's feedback frequency and support level."
- **Interactive Selection**: Single-select confidence modules. Selecting an option displays a dynamic glowing baseline, particle splash, or background color-shifting transition.
- **CTA Actions**:
  - Primary CTA: "Proceed to Calibration" (Continue).
  - Secondary CTA: "Rewind" (Back).

---

### Task: Generate At Least 5 Design Variants
You must generate at least 5 distinct futuristic, premium design concepts for this screen:
1. **Aura Spectrum Cards**: Frosted glass panels; selecting an option triggers a glowing aura color shift (amber, violet, cyan) reflecting the user's emotional state.
2. **Fluid Spectrum Slider**: A continuous fluid wave slider that changes shape, height, and color dynamically as the user slides across different confidence levels.
3. **Bento Mood Cards**: A neat dark metallic grid with high-fidelity custom icons representing writing styles, using neon borders.
4. **Holographic Soundwave Gauges**: Glowing frequency bars that pulse at different rates depending on confidence levels.
5. **Spatial Floating Pods**: Floating circular pods that swell and float closer to the user when focused.

For each variant, provide layout styles, color tokens, and dynamic transition details.
